Location

The house is located on a quiet hillside in Neochori Stoupa, surrounded by olive groves and close to both the best beaches of the peninsula and the highest mountain peaks of the Peloponnese. In less than ten minutes you can reach Kalogria, Stoupa, and Foneas beaches, while within fifteen minutes you’ll find the trailhead to the Taygetos west summit (2,407 m, UNESCO World Heritage) and the stunning Vyros Gorge. It’s an ideal base to explore the Mani Peninsula, with the Kalamata–Areopoli provincial road just five minutes away. Whether you want to immerse yourself in nature, discover Mani’s rich history, or simply relax with a glass of wine and a view, everything is close at hand.

Surroundings

The Mani peninsula is a land of striking contrasts: soaring mountains, silver olive groves, and the deep blue sea, where centuries of history live on in the stone-built villages scattered across the hills.

  • Stoupa & Agios Nikolaos: Quaint villages connected by a scenic coastal cycle path.
  • Viros Gorge: A dramatic natural wonder, with trails starting in Kardamyli, Exochori, and Tseria.
  • Diros Caves: Explore these world-famous underground caves by boat.
  • Local Beaches: From secret caves to sun-drenched shores, perfect for swimming or sunset strolls.
  • Taygetos Mountains: Ideal for hiking, climbing, or simply soaking in panoramic views.

The magic of Mani’s landscape

The Mani Peninsula is one of the few places in the world where mountains rise straight from the sea while enjoying a subtropical climate. The Taygetos range reaches over 2,400 meters, yet its foothills touch the beaches of Stoupa and Kardamyli. Within just a few kilometers, the landscape transforms from sandy shores to olive groves, citrus trees, pine forests, and rocky alpine peaks. This dramatic meeting of sea and mountain creates a rare double climate — warm and dry by the coast, cool and fragrant higher up — so you can watch snow-capped peaks while sitting in the sun by the sea.
